Second, Buzz combines news, social media, and community mentions to measure how much Gucci is being talked about — counting only the volume of mentions (20%). Third, Engagement measures how intense the reaction is per post — likes, shares, and other actual response metrics only (15%). Fourth, Share of Voice (SOV) measures how much Gucci is discussed relative to competing brands (20%).\n\nOne distinction must be underscored here: Buzz and Engagement never mix. Buzz measures \"how much\" (volume); Engagement measures \"how intensely each item resonates\" (intensity). A brand can generate many posts with lukewarm reactions, or few posts with explosive reactions — so the two components answer entirely different questions.\n\n## 3.
